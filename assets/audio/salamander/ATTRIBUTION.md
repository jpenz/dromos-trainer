# Salamander Grand Piano samples

These 17 MP3 files are a compact, single-velocity subset of **Salamander Grand
Piano V3**, recorded by Alexander Holm. They are sampled every minor third from
C2 through C6 and repitched by at most two semitones inside Dromos Trainer's
teaching range.

- Source recording: <https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano>
- Browser MP3 conversion source: <https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/>
