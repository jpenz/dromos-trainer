# Surface: the Dromos app shell and every practice page

Mode: Operate. Audience: James (Greek laiko and rebetiko bouzouki and guitar), his band, students, the public; laptop, iPad on a stand, phone; sessions of an hour or more. Job: find a tool by what you want to do, set it in one row, press one button, play along with the neck. Constraints: every feature and all 81 drills stay; Greek identity and source labels stay; zero-build vanilla JS. Cleanliness bar: joerowleymusic.co.uk/resources. Memorable moment: the red witness sign that names the dromos before the neck, and the cinnabar notes that make the dromos audible on sight.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A practice book that states its mode before every line, as a chant book does: the dromos is announced in red, then the line is played. It refuses the category default of a grey or near-black tool grid with one generic orange accent and walls of help text.

OWN-WORLD: Iron-gall ink ground (#16110d) with panels one step up and hairline rules; parchment text (#efe6d2) and a muted ink grey; cinnabar (#d6452b) only for the playing note, the primary action and the dromos's flavour notes; gold (#c9a24a) only for the tonic. GFS Didot for tool titles and the witness sign, Commissioner for all UI, tabular figures for every number. On the neck and the tab only, two muted pigments mark the dromos's lower and upper tetrachord (lapis #7b9fc9, verdigris #8fb07a), because the owner asked to see the two halves of the dromos by colour; chord tones keep their degree colours there. The chrome stays parchment, ink grey, one cinnabar and one gold. Standard pills, selects, tabs and buttons; no ornament beyond the witness sign and the red.

STORY: The player sees which tools exist, grouped by job, opens one, sees the dromos named in red at the head of the neck, sets one row of pills, presses Start and plays; explanation waits behind the Coach drawer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail grouped by job (Today; Learn the neck; Practise; Play and write; Ears and rhythm) with small caps group labels; a top context bar with Instrument, Key, Dromos, Rhythm chips; tool title in Didot with a one-line source; one row of option pills; the neck at full width directly under them, headed at its left by the red martyria cartouche (Ήχος, dromos, tonic); now and next cards and the tab strip under the neck; a bottom dock with the cinnabar Start, tempo and Autopilot pills. No paragraph above the neck. Signature interaction: the martyria updates (dromos and tonic) with a short ink settle whenever key or dromos changes, and the cinnabar flavour notes redraw on the neck.

FORM: Psaltic manuscript world, number 5 of the grounded list, rolled. seed:8b4a1733f0d9e45a

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
